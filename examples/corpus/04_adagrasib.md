# Adagrasib

Adagrasib (MRTX849) is an oral, covalent KRAS G12C inhibitor developed by Mirati Therapeutics. It is sold under the brand name Krazati.

Like Lumakras, Krazati binds the mutant cysteine of KRAS G12C. Adagrasib was evaluated in the KRYSTAL-1 trial, which included patients with KRAS G12C-mutant non-small cell lung cancer.

The FDA granted accelerated approval to adagrasib in December 2022 for adults with KRAS G12C-mutated NSCLC who had received at least one prior systemic therapy.
