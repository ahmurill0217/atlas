# CodeBreaK 100

CodeBreaK 100 was a single-arm clinical trial sponsored by Amgen. The trial evaluated AMG 510 in patients with advanced solid tumors harboring the KRAS G12C mutation.

In the lung cancer cohort, the trial enrolled patients with NSCLC who had received prior systemic therapy. Results from CodeBreaK 100 supported the accelerated approval of sotorasib by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in May 2021.

A later phase 3 trial, CodeBreaK 200, compared sotorasib with docetaxel in patients with previously treated KRAS G12C-mutated NSCLC.
