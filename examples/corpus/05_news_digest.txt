Oncology news digest (synthetic)

Amgen's Lumakras became the first approved therapy directed at a KRAS mutation when the FDA cleared it for KRAS G12C-positive non-small cell lung cancer in 2021. The approval rested on data from the CodeBreaK 100 study.

Researchers note that AMG 510 selectively inhibits KRAS G12C while sparing wild-type KRAS. Mirati's MRTX849, now called adagrasib, followed as the second KRAS G12C inhibitor to reach the market.

Both sotorasib and adagrasib are being studied in combination with other agents, including EGFR antibodies such as cetuximab in colorectal cancer.
