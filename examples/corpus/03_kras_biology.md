# KRAS biology

KRAS is a small GTPase encoded by the KRAS gene. It cycles between an active GTP-bound state and an inactive GDP-bound state and relays signals from growth factor receptors to the MAPK pathway.

Mutations in KRAS are among the most common oncogenic drivers in human cancer. The KRAS G12C variant is found in roughly 13 percent of lung adenocarcinomas, a subtype of non-small cell lung cancer.

For decades KRAS was considered undruggable. The discovery of a pocket beneath the switch II region of KRAS G12C made covalent inhibitors such as sotorasib and adagrasib possible.
