# Sotorasib overview

Sotorasib, previously known as AMG 510, is a covalent inhibitor targeting KRAS G12C. It was developed by Amgen and is marketed under the brand name Lumakras.

KRAS G12C is a mutant form of the KRAS protein in which glycine at codon 12 is replaced by cysteine. Sotorasib binds irreversibly to the cysteine residue of KRAS G12C and locks the protein in its inactive GDP-bound state.

The pivotal evidence for sotorasib came from the CodeBreaK 100 trial, a phase 2 study that enrolled patients with previously treated KRAS G12C-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).
