# KRAS G12C in colorectal cancer

KRAS G12C mutations occur in about 3 to 4 percent of colorectal cancers. In colorectal cancer, KRAS G12C inhibitor monotherapy produced lower response rates than in NSCLC, partly because of feedback activation of EGFR signaling.

The CodeBreaK 300 trial tested sotorasib combined with panitumumab, an anti-EGFR antibody, in patients with KRAS G12C-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer. Adagrasib was similarly combined with cetuximab in the KRYSTAL-1 study.
